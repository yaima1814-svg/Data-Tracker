SHOWER SCHEDULE AGREEMENT

Between: Matt and Angelo

PURPOSE

This agreement is made to keep the shower schedule clear, fair, and consistent.

AGREED RULE

NO MATTER WHAT DAY IT IS, WHAT TIME SOMEONE SHOWERS, OR WHO SHOWERS FIRST, THE SHOWER SCHEDULE WILL STILL BE FOLLOWED.

RULES

  1. Showering earlier or later does NOT change the schedule.
  2. If someone showers before their scheduled day, it does NOT automatically give them the next shower day.
  3. The next person follows the schedule exactly as written.
  4. The schedule can only be changed if BOTH Matt and Angelo agree to the change.
  5. Who ever showers first any day has to sign the paper witch is given at the end of each shower.
