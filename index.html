Rules and Regulation

The agreed shower schedule rules.

SHOWER SCHEDULE AGREEMENT

Between: Matt and Angelo

Purpose: To keep the shower schedule clear, fair, and consistent.

RULES AND REGULATION

  1. The schedule must still be followed, no matter what day or time someone showers first.
  2. Showering earlier or later does not change the schedule.
  3. Showering before your scheduled day does not automatically give you the next shower day.
  4. The next person follows the established schedule.
  5. The schedule may only be changed when both Matt and Angelo agree.
  6. The person who showers first signs the paper provided at the end of the shower.
  7. The calendar is the record of the agreed schedule. Check it instead of assuming the schedule has changed.

Both people agree to follow these rules and communicate respectfully about any requested changes.
